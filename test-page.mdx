### How to check Custom Attribution eligibility with Retool

**🚦 TL;DR: If `CAS Status` is ✓Ready, the advertiser is ready to set up custom attribution campaigns on the Meta side.**

Retool columns:

1. `Company name`
2. `Client ID` - dashboard ID
3. `CSM`
4. `MS`
5. `CAS Status` - is the customer ready to test? (this must show ready for us to track tests post-launch)
6. `Action responsible` - who is responsible for next steps?
7. `Next step` - what are the next steps?
8. `Apex consent` - have they opted into Apex within Northbeam settings?
9. `North Star` - must be `Accrual` and `1 day` or `7 day`
10. `Estimated daily attributed transactions` - minimum is 15 total transactions per day (including fractional credit, total volume must add up to 15)
11. `Pixel configuration` - whether the customer is on single or multi-pixel.
12. `Configured CAS` - name of the Custom Attribution Source tied to the pixel
13. Has correct pixel permissions - has the customer successfully reconnected their Meta accounts?
14. `First event sent at` - when data started being sent to the configured pixel (does not always mean the day apex was configured)
15. `Expected date for model readiness` - 15 days after "first event sent at"
16. `Data quality diagnostics` - Lets you know if a customer is seeing an error in their Meta UI.
17. `Is model ready for optimization` - for multi-pixel clients, this is the source of truth, check = customer is fully configured, data is trained and good, and Custom Attribution should be available in the customers Meta UI.
