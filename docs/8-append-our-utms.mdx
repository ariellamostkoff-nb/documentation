# What is a UTM?

UTM stands for the "Urchin Tracking Module". Urchin was a web analytics software, acquired by Google in 2005 to create Google Analytics. UTM parameters are added to a URL to track web traffic stemming from a specific source (social ad, email, display ad, etc.)

## Why do I need to configure UTMs?

Northbeam uses UTMs to link your metrics to specific ads, building detailed customer journeys.

<Warning>
  Please Note:

  **Northbeam UTMs are required for active ads on integrated platforms** to ensure accurate spend mapping and attribution. Attribution tracking won’t be available until Northbeam UTMs are added to your active ads on these platforms.
</Warning>
