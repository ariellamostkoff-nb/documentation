## Supported Attribution Models

The ROKT integration currently supports the following Northbeam attribution models:

* First Touch
* Last Touch
* Last Non-Direct Touch
* Clicks-Only
* Clicks + Modeled Views (C+MV)

***

## **Step 1: Enter Your App ID and App Secret**

### 1A. Generate your API credentials in ROKT

1. Sign in to **One Platform** at `my.rokt.com`.
2. Navigate to **Profile Settings** (under your account icon at the bottom left).
3. Scroll to the **Generate Personal API Credentials** section.
4. Enter a name for your app and click **Generate**.

This will generate an **App ID** and **App Secret** to plug into Northbeam.

<Tip>
  Tips

  * The app name can be anything. It is only used for internal reference (example: `Northbeam`).
  * Store the **App Secret** securely. You will not have access to the App Secret again after leaving the page.
</Tip>

<Note>
  ROKT documentation: [https://docs.rokt.com/developers/api-reference/reporting/query-api/#generating-a-rokt-app-id-and-app-secret](https://docs.rokt.com/developers/api-reference/reporting/query-api/#generating-a-rokt-app-id-and-app-secret)
</Note>

<br />

### 1B. Enter credentials in Northbeam

1. In Northbeam, go to **Settings → Connections → ROKT**.
2. Paste your **App ID** and **App Secret**.
3. Click **Connect**.

## Step 2: Insert UTM Parameters

Reach out to your **ROKT account lead** and they will inject the necessary Northbeam UTM parameters for you.

Once the connection is live and UTM parameters are appended, you're all set!
