The delta is unique to your business and empowers directional decision-making. A low number in Northbeam does not necessarily mean low performance.

What’s important here is correlating your platform/ ad set/ ad level data to your top-line business performance over a given period to determine whether the numbers are “good” or “bad”.

We'd recommend Profitability Benchmarks to understand [what is a good/bad number](/docs/what-is-a-good-bad-number-in-northbeam).
