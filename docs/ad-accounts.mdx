---
title: "Adding / Removing Ad Accounts"
---

# Adding extra ad accounts

1. Go to **Settings › Connections**.
2. Find the platform whose account you want to add (e.g. Meta, Google) and open its connection.
3. Select **Add account** and complete the platform's authorization flow.
4. Confirm the new account appears as **Connected**. Spend and metrics for that account begin flowing on the next data refresh.

# Removing an ad account

1. Go to **Settings › Connections** and open the platform's connection.
2. Select the account you want to remove, then choose **Remove**.
3. The account stops syncing new data. Historical data already attributed remains in your dashboard.
