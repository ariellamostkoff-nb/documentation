title: "What is Northbeam's MTA Methodology?"
description: "What makes Northbeam's MTA unique?"

Northbeam is an unbiased multi-touch attribution (MTA) solution. We are not incentivized to attribute to any specific channel, and that is the key difference from most ad platforms.

Here is how we do it:

* Rely on first-party data
* Fractionalizes transactions and revenue across all relevant touchpoints, and
* Show which marketing activities are driving profitable results.
