# Step 1: Insert Northbeam UTM Parameters

Please add the following UTM parameters in the **Final URL Suffix** at the **Account Level**.

```
nbt=nb%3Aadwords%3A{network}%3A{campaignid}%3A{adgroupid}%3A{creative}
```

<Note>
  ## UTM Parameter Priority

  Google Ads applies UTM parameters based on hierarchy. **Lower-level UTMs always take priority over higher-level UTMs.**

  Priority order, highest to lowest:

  1. **Ad level**
  2. **Ad group level**
  3. **Campaign level**
  4. **Account level**

  This means that if UTMs exist at the campaign, ad group, or ad level, they will **override** anything set at the account-level Final URL Suffix.

  ### What if I already have UTMs at the Campaign, Ad Group or Ad level?

  You have two valid options:

  * **Move your existing UTMs up** to the account level to centralize tracking
  * **Move Northbeam UTMs down** to the same level as your existing UTMs

  Either approach works as long as UTMs are applied consistently and not overridden at a lower level.

  If UTMs exist at multiple levels, Google will always respect the lowest-level definition.
</Note>

## Have existing UTM parameters?

If you have existing parameters, please add our parameters to the end (or beginning) and we'll all set. Be sure to use an ampersand (&) to connect your existing parameters to our parameters.

For example:

utm\_source=paid\&utm\_medium=google&**nbt=nb%3Aadwords%3A\{network}%3A\{campaignid}%3A\{adgroupid}%3A\{creative}**

<br />

<Note>
  UTM Auto-Appending

  If you would like Northbeam to automatically append UTM parameters to all Google ads from ad accounts connected to your dashboard, please [reach out to our Support team](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) to enable this feature.
</Note>

# Step 2: Connect Your Google Ads Account

## What does the connection do?

Northbeam natively integrates with the Google Ads API. By doing so, we pull all your current and historical Campaigns, Adsets, and Ads from your account, along with a list of useful metrics.  Notably, we pull impressions, which fuel our view model and unlock our Clicks + Modeled Views attribution model. We also pull ad spend to help you with decision making.

<br />

<Tip>
  ## Important Connection Requirements

  Before connecting your Google Ads account, please ensure the following requirements are met. Without these, the integration cannot complete successfully:

  1. **The user connecting the account must have Admin access.**

  Admin-level permissions are required for Northbeam to read campaigns, ad groups, ads, and key reporting metrics.

  2. **An Account Name must be set in Google Ads.**

  Go to _Admin > Account Settings > Account Name_ and make sure a valid name is defined. Accounts without a name cannot be synced into Northbeam.

  If either requirement is missing, the connection will fail or result in incomplete data flowing into your dashboard.
</Tip>

<br />

## How to connect your Ad Account

* **Onboarding Flow** - If you're in our onboarding flow, please navigate to **Integrations**
* **Activated Dashboard** - If your dashboard is activated, please go to **Settings > Connections**

### Adding an Extra Google Ads Account

Google Ads lets you manage multiple ad accounts under one **Manager Account (MCC)**. Before adding an extra account in Northbeam, you'll need your Manager Account ID handy.

<Warning>
  **Before you start:**

  * All Google Ads accounts you want to connect to a single Northbeam dashboard must be under the same Manager Account.
  * The user shown next to **Connected Account** in your [Connections Page](https://dashboard.northbeam.io/settings/connections) is the one who authenticated the integration. This person must have **Admin access** to both the Manager Account and each individual account being added. Without this, the connection will fail.

### Step 2: Update the MCC ID in Northbeam

1. Go to **Settings > Connections > Google Ads**
2. In the field labeled **"Update your login customer id (parent MCC account)"**, replace the existing value with your MCC Account ID.
3. Click **Save**
4. Once saved, click **Add another account** to expand the search panel
5. Search for and select the account you want to add
6. Confirm the connection

## 2. Why do I see a data discrepancy?

**Discrepancies in Revenue, Orders, and Visits are expected.** The reason for this lies in the differences between Northbeam's attribution model and the corresponding platform's attribution model. Each attribution model has a unique method of allocating credit.

For example, Northbeam's Clicks-Only model divides credit across all touchpoints and tries to push credit to your upper funnel channels. Let's say your in-platform model uses Last Click, which gives credit to the last touchpoint in the journey. This is going to cause a discrepancy.

<DiscrepancyExplanation />

### Attribution Models and Windows

**Google:**

* Attribution Model: Last Click (default)
* Attribution Window: 30-Day Click (default)
  * [Source: Google Conversion Windows](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3123169?hl=en)

**Northbeam:**

* Attribution Model: Varies (default is Clicks-Only)
* Attribution Window: 1-Day (default, can extend to an infinite lifetime value)
