# Setting up Bliss Point by Tinuiti within Northbeam involves two steps:

Step 1: Get AWS access keys and S3 Bucket Name

Step 2: Share this information with our Support Team

# Step 1: Get AWS access keys and S3 Bucket Name

Please log into your Bliss Point Media account and find the following:

* AWS Programmatic Access Keys
* S3 Bucket Name

# Step 2: Share with our Support Team

Please [reach out to our Support Team](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) with your AWS Keys and S3 Bucket Name.

We'll plug this into our backend and enable our integration!
