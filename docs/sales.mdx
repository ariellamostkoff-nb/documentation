## What is the Sales page for?

This Sales page is the most utilized page on Northbeam.

Here, you have a complete picture of how your marketing is performing across all channels and you can do your daily campaign optimization.

As you scroll, you will see a chart that shows each of your channels as well as the key metrics associated with each one. This is an overview of each platform that you can sort on the Campaign level, Adset level, and even Ad level. This view is similar to the UI that you typically see inside your ad manager platforms:

* Spend
* Revenue (1st Time)
* ROAS (1st Time)
* New Customers
* CAC (1st Time)

You can customize your table with any metrics that you have utilized in the past, or metrics that you are familiar with. Once you have added your new metric, your chart will update retroactively.

<Warning>
  You can learn more about these metrics and other metrics [here](/docs/northbeam-metrics-101).
</Warning>
