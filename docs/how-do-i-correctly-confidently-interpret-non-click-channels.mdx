Attributing performance to **Non-Click Channels** like TV, Podcast, or Influencer campaigns can be difficult, but Northbeam makes it easier.

Our [Metrics Explorer](/docs/metrics-explorer) help you uncover the **indirect impact** of these channels by showing correlation trends between spend and key business outcomes (Revenue or First-Time Purchases). You can explore this data with custom time ranges and attribution models on granular views.

For example, you can use [Metrics Explorer](https://dashboard.northbeam.io/metrics-explorer) to reveal a strong correlation between TV Spend and First-Time Transaction over the last 30 days to see the true value of offline investments:
