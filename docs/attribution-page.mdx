The **Attribution Home Page** is your central hub for daily actions and changes.

Here, your team of media buyers, channel managers, and any related-to-platform team can decide which platforms, campaigns, ads, and assets to adjust and improve for better performance.

Your default setting on this page is **Attribution**: *Revenue*, which shows the following:

* Sales
* New Customers
* Returning Customers
* Top of Funnel (Demand Capture)
* Bottom of Funnel (Demand Generation)
* Organic and Owned Media

<Warning>
  On the right side of your screen, you will see Profitability, which cannot be utilized until you have passed the 90-day learning period with Northbeam. Learn more about [Profitability Benchmarks here](/docs/profitability-benchmarks).
</Warning>

You will also see the other tabs that contain:

* *Product Analytics*
* *Orders*
* *Creative Analytics*
