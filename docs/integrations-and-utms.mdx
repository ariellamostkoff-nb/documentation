## UTMs

UTM stands for "Urchin Tracking Module" — parameters added to a URL to track the source of web traffic (a social ad, email, display ad, and so on). Northbeam uses UTMs to link your metrics to specific ads and build detailed customer journeys.

<Warning>
  **Northbeam UTMs are required for active ads on integrated platforms** to ensure accurate spend mapping and attribution. Attribution tracking won't be available until Northbeam UTMs are added to your active ads on these platforms.
</Warning>
