---
title: "9. Integrations and UTMs"
description: "Connect Integrations and UTMs"
---

Connect your platform accounts to Northbeam, and if you’re prompted, please grant Northbeam the highest level of permissions requested for each platform. These connections are essential to give you a complete view of your advertising performance.

Northbeam has native integrations with:

* [Facebook Ads](/docs/setting-up-facebook-ads-tracking)
* [Google Ads](/docs/setting-up-google-ads-for-northbeam)
* [Amazon Ads](/docs/tracking-for-amazon)
* [Amazon Seller](/docs/tracking-for-amazon)
* [Microsoft Ads](/docs/tracking-for-microsoft)
* [Snapchat Ads](/docs/tracking-for-snapchat-ads)
* [Pinterest Ads](/docs/tracking-for-pinterest-ads)
* [TitkTok Ads](/docs/setting-up-tiktok-ads-for-northbeam)

Additional connections include:

* [Attentive](/docs/working-tracking-for-attentive)
* [MNTN](/docs/tracking-for-mntn)
* [Tatari](/docs/tracking-for-tatari)
* [Rakuten](/docs/tracking-for-rakuten)
* [Bliss Point Media](/docs/tracking-for-bliss-point-by-tinuiti)

<Note>
  ### Don’t see one of your platforms or services listed here?

  Reference our [Tracking for Non-Integrated Channels guide](/docs/tracking-for-other-platforms) to learn more about options and requirements.

  [Reach out to our Support Team](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) for additional guidance.
</Note>
