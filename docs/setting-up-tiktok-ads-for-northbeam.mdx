# Step 1: Connect Your TikTok Ad Account

## What does the connection do?

Northbeam natively integrates with the TikTok Ads API. By doing so, we pull all your current and historical Campaigns, Adsets, and Ads from your account, along with a list of useful metrics.  Notably, we pull impressions, which fuel our view model and unlock our Clicks + Modeled Views attribution model. We also pull ad spend and list of in-platform conversion & creative metrics to help you with decision making.

## How to connect your Ad Account

How to connect:

* **Onboarding Flow** - If you're in our onboarding flow, please navigate to **Integrations**
* **Activated Dashboard** - If your dashboard is activated, please go to **Settings > Connections**

<Warning>
  Note: The credentials used must have admin access to the Ad Account.
</Warning>

# GMV Max Campaigns

TikTok’s GMV Max is an automated advertising solution designed to maximize a seller’s total sales revenue (GMV) within their TikTok Shop. It uses AI to optimize campaign performance — automating tasks like audience targeting, bidding, and creative selection — while focusing on hitting a target ROI and driving incremental sales through a mix of paid and organic traffic.

In short, GMV Max takes over the heavy lifting of campaign management so you can scale your TikTok Shop sales more efficiently.

## How to track GMV Max in Northbeam:

**Requirements**

* Your TikTok Shop account must be connected to a TikTok Ad Account
* That TikTok Ad Account must also be connected to Northbeam
* The connected user must be an admin on the TikTok Ad Account and TikTok Shops account.

<Tip>
  Once these connections are in place, Northbeam will automatically attribute GMV Max campaign data to the corresponding TikTok campaigns.
</Tip>

## Where to Find GMV Max Performance in Northbeam

Navigate to the Sales page in Northbeam. You'll see a platform labeled **GMV Max**. Within this platform, you’ll find your GMV Max campaigns along with attributed conversion data.

## Troubleshooting GMV Max

**If you don’t see GMV Max spend or attributed conversion data, it’s likely due to one of the following:**

* Your TikTok Shop account isn’t connected to any TikTok Ad Account
* Your TikTok Shop account is connected to a TikTok Ad Account that isn't linked to Northbeam

**To verify this setup in TikTok Business Center:**

1. Select the correct Business Center
2. Go to Assets → Ad Accounts
3. Select the relevant Ad Account
4. Navigate to Assets → TikTok Shop
   1. If your shop isn’t listed, it’s not linked to that Ad Account

# TikTok Smart+ 2.0 (Smart Performance Campaigns)

TikTok Smart+ 2.0 adds a new level called **Asset Groups**, which sits between Ad Groups and creatives.

Because this structure is different from standard TikTok campaigns, Northbeam made an update so Asset Group names are easy to see in reporting.

## How Smart+ 2.0 Appears in Northbeam

**TikTok Smart+ 2.0 structure:**

* Campaign
* Ad Group
* **Asset Group**
* Creative

**How Northbeam Displays It**

* Campaign
* Ad Group
* **Asset Group |:| Creative Name**

Northbeam shows the Asset Group **at the start of the Ad name**, followed by the creative name.

### Ad Name Format

`Asset Group Name |:| Creative Name`

**Example:**

`Prospecting - UGC |:| spark-echo_V3_QRT472_LD-20251112.mp4`

**What to know**

* `|:|` is a separator between the Asset Group and the Creative
* This happens automatically for Smart+ 2.0 campaigns
* No setup or changes are required

## How to View Performance by Asset Group

Asset Groups are not a separate tab yet, but you can still review performance by using search.

**Steps**:

1. Go to the **Sales** Page
2. Filter Platform to **TikTok**
3. At the **Ad level**, use the search bar to enter the Asset Group name

This will show all ads that belong to that Asset Group.

<Note>
  This update improves naming clarity for Smart+ 2.0 campaigns. More Asset Group reporting improvements are being explored.
</Note>

## 3. Why do I see a data discrepancy?

**Discrepancies in Revenue and Orders are expected.** The reason for this lies in the differences between Northbeam's attribution model and the corresponding platform's attribution model. Each attribution model has a unique method of allocating credit.

For example, Northbeam's Clicks-Only model divides credit across all touchpoints and tries to push credit to your upper funnel channels. Let's say your in-platform model uses Last Click, which gives credit to the last touchpoint in the journey. This is going to cause a discrepancy.

<DiscrepancyExplanation />

### Attribution Models and Windows

**TikTok:**

* Attribution Model: Last Click
* Attribution Window: 7-Day Click/1-Day View
  * [Source: TikTok Attribution Settings](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/attribution-overview?lang=en)

**Northbeam:**

* Attribution Model: Varies (default is Clicks-Only)
* Attribution Window: 1-Day (default, can extend to an infinite lifetime value)
