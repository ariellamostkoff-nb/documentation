---

* You can reference **Discount Codes** on your [Customer LTV](https://dashboard.northbeam.io/customers) page, which reflects the average lifetime value of each code.

To analyze performance effectively, it's best to use **Clicks-Only** or **First-Touch** (this is only for advanced users).

**Here are the key metrics** you should keep an eye on:

* Attributed Revenue
* E-commerce Conversion Rate (ECR)
* New Visits
* New Customer %

[Custom Labels](/docs/manage-breakdowns) are also essential for grouping traffic by the specific influencer to measure impact effectively.

With proper labeling and attribution setup, you can confidently track influencer-driven performance—even when click data is limited.

Please reach out to your Media Strategist, Customer Success Manager, or contact [Support](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) if you have any questions.
