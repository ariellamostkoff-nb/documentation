---
title: "5. Add Domain"
description: "Set your top-level domain for your dashboard"
---

Please enter the **top-level domain** of your website, such as `northbeam.io`, `google.com`, or `bbc.co.uk`.

<Warning>
  Setup Requirements

  **If your business operates multiple storefronts across different domains or subdomains** (e.g., `mystore.com`, `us.mystore.com`, `mystore.co.uk`), please [submit a support ticket](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) for assistance. Read more about multi-domain configurations [here](/docs/multi-domain-configuration).
</Warning>
