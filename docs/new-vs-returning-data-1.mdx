Northbeam is able to identify New and Returning visitors and orders. We offer three different kinds of New and Returning data:

* First-Time and Returning **Visitors**
* First-Time and Returning **Customers**
* First-Time and Recurring **Subscriptions**

| Type                                       | How we define                    | Example Associated Metrics                                                                                       |
| :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First-Time and Returning **Visitors**      | Northbeam Pixel and Device Graph | New Visits, Visits                                                                                               |
| First-Time and Returning **Customers**     | Customer ID                      | Transactions (1st Time), Transactions (Returning), Attributed Revenue (1st Time), Attributed Revenue (Returning) |
| First-Time and Recurring **Subscriptions** | Order Tags                       | Subscriptions (1st Time), Subscriptions (Recurring)                                                              |

## First-Time vs Returning Visitors

Our **Northbeam Pixel** uses a variety of inputs to create user profiles and track customer journeys across various devices and households. All of this information feeds into our backend device graph.

* **First-Time Visitor** - The Northbeam Pixel did not recognize any previous events from this user
* **Returning Visitor** - The Northbeam Pixel recognized previous event(s) from this user

## First-Time and Returning Customers

New and Returning Customers are based on **Customer IDs** and timestamps associated to each order.

* **First-Time Customer** - When an order has a Customer ID that doesn't exist on a historical order in Northbeam
* **Returning Customer** - When an order has a Customer ID that exists on a historical order in Northbeam

| Order Number | Date of Order | Customer ID | Customer Type | Reasoning for Customer Type               |
| :----------- | :------------ | :---------- | :------------ | :---------------------------------------- |
| 01           | Jan 1, 2024   | 1000        | First Time    | ID doesn't exist on a previous order.     |
| 02           | Jan 1, 2024   | 1001        | First Time    | ID doesn't exist on a previous order.     |
| 03           | Jan 2, 2024   | 1000        | Returning     | ID exists on a previous order (Order 01). |
| 04           | Jan 3, 2024   | 1002        | First Time    | ID doesn't exist on a previous order.     |
| 05           | Jan 4, 2024   | 1001        | Returning     | ID exists on a previous order (Order 02). |

<Note>
  For the most accurate New and Returning data...

  1. **Backfill Historical Order Data** - It's important to backfill as much order data as possible so we have your entire customer base, along with their IDs.
  1. *Note: This is primarily relevant for customers who are on a Custom setup (Shopify customers should already have historical data pulled in).*
  2. **Consistent Customer ID formats** - Ensure that all of your Customer IDs are in a consistent format. This way could properly identify New and Returning Customers, or IDs.
  1. *Note: Also primarily relevant for customers on a Custom setup (i.e. Shopify customers have Customer IDs automatically pulled in).*
</Note>
