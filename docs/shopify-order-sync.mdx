**Step 8a** - Connect your Shopify store

<Frame>
  <img src="https://files.readme.io/2ad1255d299f6d4eacb76967553b972dd029604640b7e4debf713857ae9284ae-image.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

If you are not logged in to your Shopify store, you will be redirected to the Shopify App store to [download the Northbeam App](https://apps.shopify.com/north-beam)

<Frame>
  <img src="https://files.readme.io/fa9bdbf21b577ae955cb4b83319ca043996aae18c306c54b9e8de32e699c358d-image.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

You'll be prompted to login to your Shopify store

<Frame>
  <img src="https://files.readme.io/15327439fdc5eba82189dee674df754a1011326f0583bb5fa8f4024929c8b6b3-image.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

Select your Shopify store from the menu

![](https://files.readme.io/f7f60bf737947a9a668ea5a6c938b4483fad10c52df4deaa86421fb391e27c6f-image.png)

Once connected you will be redirected back to the Northbeam onboarding flow where you will see your Shopify store name and you can move forward with the remaining steps in step 8.
