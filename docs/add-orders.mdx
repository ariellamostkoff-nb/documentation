# Background

For Northbeam's Multi-Touch Attribution (MTA) and Mixed Media Modeling (MMM) offerings, we use our customers' Orders as the ground truth for Revenue. This enables Northbeam to perform Ad Attribution on Purchases that took place (MTA) and to assist in predicting what will take place (MMM).

***

## There are 2 ways to send your order data to Northbeam:

1. [Shopify Connector ](/docs/shopify-order-sync)(If your backend orders management system is hosted on Shopify)
2. [Orders API](/docs/orders-api)(If your backend orders management system is NOT Shopify)
