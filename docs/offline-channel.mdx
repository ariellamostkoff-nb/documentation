---
title: "🌚 Offline Channel"
sidebarTitle: "Offline Channel"
description: "Offline Channels (like Podcasts, Linear, DOOH, Direct Mail, and CTV) do not generate Clicks, so Northbeam can't directly attribute conversions to them the way it does for digital channels."
---

Instead, we can use correlative analysis, aka our [Metrics Explorer](/docs/metrics-explorer), to understand the potential impact of offline spend.

* Metric Explorer looks for patterns and relationships between offline spend and performance metrics like **First-Time Revenue**. This is a correlation analysis, not causation.
* For example, if you spend $30,000 on a TV Ad, Northbeam may identify a lift in First-Time Revenue **proportional growth** over that time period and suggest a **possible relationship**.
* You can also use [Custom Labels](/docs/manage-breakdowns) and Regex (Regular Expressions) to separate and analyze different versions of TV Ads in their own campaigns.
  * Isolate TV Ad *Version A* vs. *Version B* to measure each one's specific impact on First-Time Revenue.
* Even if TV Spend does not directly increase First-Time Revenue, you might see a **lift** in other digital channels like Google or Meta. This is known as the **halo effect**, where spend in one channel indirectly boosts attributed performance in another.

While attribution is limited, spend efficiency and performance trends can still be analyzed to inform decisions.
