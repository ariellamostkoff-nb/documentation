1. Navigate to the **Attribution Page**
2. Click **Customize ⚙️**

From here, you'll see a list of all the metrics we provide and you'll be able to add/remove any from your view:

# Northbeam Conversion Metrics

Conversion metics are by far the most commonly used metrics within Northbeam.

**Note: All conversion metrics in Accrual Accounting mode are reported in an Attribution Window, which is denoted in the metric name.**

Examples:

* Transactions (1d) - Transactions completed in a 1-day window
* Attributed Rev (1d) - Revenue in a 1-day window
* ROAS (7d) - Return on Ad Spend calculated with Revenue in a 7-day window

**Learn more about our[Attribution Windows here](/docs/attribution-windows).**

# Northbeam Click Metrics

Below are Click-Based metrics. Keep in mind, we use Visits when calculating these formulas, not Clicks.

<Table align={["left","left","left"]}>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>
        Metric Name
      </th>

      <th>
        Definition
      </th>

      <th>
        Calculation or Notes
      </th>
    </tr>
  </thead>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>
        Visits
      </td>

      <td>
        Visits to your website
      </td>

      <td>
        Tracked via Northbeam Pixel.

        Visits are different from Clicks. Not all Clicks result in a Visit, so Visits are usually lower than in-platform Clicks.
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        New Visits
      </td>

      <td>
        Visits to your website from new visitors
      </td>

      <td>
        New Visitors are determined by the Northbeam Pixel and our device graph.
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        % New Visits
      </td>

      <td>
        Percentage of first-time website visitors
      </td>

      <td>
        (New Visits ÷ Visits) × 100
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        CPM
      </td>

      <td>
        Cost Per Mille, or Cost per 1000 Impressions
      </td>

      <td>
        (Spend ÷ Impressions) × 1000
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        CTR
      </td>

      <td>
        Click-Thru Rate
      </td>

      <td>
        Visits ÷ Impressions

        We use Visits in this formula, not Clicks.
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        eCPC
      </td>

      <td>
        Cost per Visit
      </td>

      <td>
        Spend ÷ Visits

        We use Visits in this formula, not Clicks.
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        eCPNV
      </td>

      <td>
        Cost per New Visit
      </td>

      <td>
        Spend ÷ New Visits
      </td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</Table>
