**Best for:** creative and growth teams deciding what to iterate, scale, or kill.

## The steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Creative Analytics">
    Performance by creative, across your channels.
    [Creative Analytics →](/docs/creative-analytics)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold the measurement constant">
    Same model, window, and accounting mode across every creative you compare.
    [Attribution windows →](/docs/attribution-windows)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the volume before you judge">
    Low-spend creatives produce noisy numbers — read them accordingly.
    [Good vs. bad numbers →](/docs/what-is-a-good-bad-number-in-northbeam)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to expect

Creative decisions compound: small, consistent evaluation beats occasional deep dives.
