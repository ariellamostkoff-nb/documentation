**Best for:** whoever owns the Monday number.

## The steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the breakdowns you'll report on">
    Group channels and campaigns the way your team talks about them.
    [Manage breakdowns →](/docs/manage-breakdowns)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix the reporting frame">
    Pick the model, window, and accounting mode once — and keep them stable week to week.
    [Accounting modes →](/docs/accounting-modes)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context with benchmarks">
    Compare your numbers against profitability benchmarks.
    [Profitability benchmarks →](/docs/profitability-benchmarks)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the data">
    Pull the tables you need for your deck or sheet.
    [Exporting data →](/docs/exporting-data)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to expect

A stable frame is what makes week-over-week movement meaningful — change the model or window and you've changed the story.
