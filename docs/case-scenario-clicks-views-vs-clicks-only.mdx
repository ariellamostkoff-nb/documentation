# Why are my numbers good on Clicks + Modeled Views but bad on Clicks-Only?

Our **Clicks-Only** attribution model only attributes credit to **factual** clicks. While extremely valuable, it under-values businesses and ads that rely more heavily on view-based attribution.

Since platforms do not pass view-level data to Northbeam, our proprietary machine-learning model takes into consideration your factual click-level data and its correlation with organic touchpoints to attribute view-based credit where appropriate.

We've built [Clicks + Deterministic Views](/docs/clicks-deterministic-views), which ingest impressions data from TikTok and AppLovin that relies on deterministic modeling, versus probabilistic (Clicks-Only).

# Is there a normal/standard delta between Clicks + Modeled Views and Clicks-Only?

Much like comparing the cash/accrual delta, there is **not a standard delta** between metrics resulting from viewing our Clicks + Modeled Views and Clicks-Only attribution models.
