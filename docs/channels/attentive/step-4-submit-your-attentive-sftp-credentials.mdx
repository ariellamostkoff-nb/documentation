After your data feed is set up, enter your credentials in the Connections Page. Your credentials should contain an email address and password.

1. Click **Settings**
2. On the left navigation, click **Connections**
3. Scroll down to **Attentive**
4. Enter **Username and Password**
5. Click **Connect**
