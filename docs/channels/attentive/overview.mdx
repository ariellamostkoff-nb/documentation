* Visits, revenue, and orders from Attentive Campaigns and Flows
* Email and SMS sign-up events from Attentive’s opt-in forms

| Input                            | If Input is missing...                                                      | Output                                                                                                       |
| :------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Attentive Integration (required) | Sign up volume will be missing and associated metrics will not be populated | Sign up event volume is imported from Attentive to your Northbeam dashboard.                                 |
| UTM Parameters (required)        | Visits and Revenue will not be attributed to your Attentive Campaigns       | Visits and Revenue attribution to Attentive Campaigns(ex. "Welcome Series" drove $X in revenue)              |
| Sign Up Event                    | Sign ups will be Unattributed                                               | Sign Up attribution to all channels(ex. Facebook drove X amount of Sign Ups)                                 |
| Custom Spendsheet                | There will be no costs associated with Attentive                            | Spend/Cost data associated to Attentive.Required for cost-based metrics associated Attentive (ex. ROAS, CAC) |
