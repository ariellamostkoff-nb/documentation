## Step 4: Allow 24 Hours for the Tatari Connection to Process


After entering your Tatari ID and selecting the source of truth in Northbeam, please allow up to 24 hours for the integration to fully process.

<Warning>
  Attempting to connect your AWS Credentials too early can result in an error:

  ![API Error 400: Invalid ARNs](https://files.readme.io/46aed383f3101466cd1f92dbd0a607d134ea0e757cd1db907911e9e2de54185b-image_117.png)

  "API Error 400: Invalid ARNs"

  This is expected behavior if the connection has not yet completed. Simply wait and retry after the 24-hour window.
</Warning>

Once the connection is live and UTMs are on your ads, confirm data is
flowing:

* **UTMs captured** — follow [Checking UTM tracking](/docs/checking-utm-tracking)
  to confirm Northbeam parameters are present on your landing pages.
* **Connection syncing** — follow
  [Checking platform connections](/docs/checking-api-connections) to confirm
  the account shows connected and data is arriving.

If either check fails, see
[Troubleshooting tracking issues](/docs/troubleshooting-tracking-issues).
