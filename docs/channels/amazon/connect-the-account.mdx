## Step 1: Connect Your Amazon Accounts


### What do the connections do?

Northbeam natively integrates with the Amazon Seller and Amazon Ads API. By doing so, we pull all your PPC spend and revenue from your Amazon Marketplaces.

### How to connect your Ad Account

Please be sure to connect your **Amazon Seller Account and Amazon Ads Account**. Amazon Seller fetches your store's revenue, while Amazon Ads fetches your store's spend.

How to connect:

* **Onboarding Flow** - If you're in our onboarding flow, please navigate to **Integrations**
* **Activated Dashboard** - If your dashboard is activated, please go to **Settings > Connections**

<Warning>
  Note: The credentials used must have admin access to the Ad Account.
</Warning>
