Retrieve the information needed to set up your integration by navigating to the Connections menu in your dashboard. Follow these steps:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Select **Connections**
3. Scroll down to find **TradeDesk**

You'll find the following:

* **TradeDesk Channels** - These are the  Tradedesk channels that are available. Keep in mind, any view-only channels are not measurable at the time (Audio, Digital, Out of Home).
* **Click UTMs** – URLs to be used in Click Tracking.
* **Email for Scheduled Report** – The email address for sending scheduled reports.
