---
title: "Step 3: Enable Rakuten Integration"
sidebarTitle: "3. Enable Rakuten Integration"
icon: "file-lines"
---

1. Navigate to the **Connections** menu in Northbeam:
   1. Go to **Settings**
   2. Select **Connections**
   3. Scroll down to locate **Rakuten**
2. In the **Connections** menu, check the boxes to confirm that the following have been set up:
   1. Click UTMs
   2. Scheduled Report
3. Click **Connect**
