## Step 1: Enter Your API Key


To connect your Vibe.co account:

1. Log in to your **Vibe.co account**.
2. Generate your**API Key**: https://help.vibe.co/en/articles/8943325-vibe-api-reporting
3. In Northbeam, go to **Settings → Connections → Vibe.co**.
4. Paste your **API key** and click **Connect**.

### Prerequisites for CDV

* The Vibe.co API key must be linked to an account with an active Vibe campaign. You cannot enable the integration until a campaign is live.
* To verify readiness, ping the Vibe.co API endpoint (`/get_advertiser_ids`). It will return an advertiser ID only after campaign activation (usually within 30 minutes of launch).

***

## Step 2: Enable Clicks + Deterministic Views (C+DV)


Once your Vibe.co connection is active in Northbeam:

1. Go to **Settings → Model Settings → Attribution Models**.
2. Select **Clicks + Deterministic Views (C+DV)** from the list of attribution models.
3. Under Available Platforms, check the box for **Vibe.co**.
4. Click **Save** **Changes**.
5. Allow up to 48 hours for Vibe.co impression data to populate in your dashboard.
