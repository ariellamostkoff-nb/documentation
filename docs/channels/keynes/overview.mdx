**This guide will show you how to set up Keynes spend tracking in Northbeam.**

Once connected, Northbeam will automatically pull:

* Spend data from Keynes
* Campaign-level reporting data from Keynes

Because Keynes is a CTV platform, this integration is currently **spend-only** and does not support click attribution, UTMs, or revenue attribution.
