## 1. How do you track and attribute TikTok Shops conversions?

Northbeam identifies each individual TikTok Shops purchase and fractionalizes purchase credit across all your TikTok campaigns.

<Note>
  Note:

  **The Northbeam Pixel doesn't fire on TikTok Shops storefronts, so we don't track Visits.** Consequently, the Visits count won't be recorded, and CPCs (Cost Per Clicks) may appear inflated for any Campaigns, Ad Sets, or Ads leading to TikTok Shops.
</Note>

## 2. How can I view TikTok Shops ad performance?

TikTok Shops purchase credit is seamlessly factored into your Attributed Revenue and Transactions metric columns.

To see the revenue or transaction breakout across Web and Shops orders:

1. Hover over any cell in the **Attributed Rev** or **Transactions** columns:

## 3. How can I view TikTok Shops organic performance?

Sometimes customers visit your Shops platform to make direct purchases without having clicked on an ad - we refer to these as Organic purchases. Northbeam uses a combination of statistics, platform data, and your ad’s performance to produce an estimate of Organic purchases made on your Shops storefront.

You can see Organic Shops data using the **TikTok Shops (Organic)** Platform label.
