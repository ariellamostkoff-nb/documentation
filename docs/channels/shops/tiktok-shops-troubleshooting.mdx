* **Ad spend appears but no GMV/revenue:**
  * Confirm the Shop is **bound in Ads Manager** (not Business Center).
  * Confirm the TikTok user is an **Admin** on both the Shop and advertiser account.
  * Confirm the TikTok advertiser account is properly connected to Northbeam.
  * For Shopify Stores
    * Make sure your store is connected to your shop via [TikTok Shops](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/tiktok/setup) app
  * For Custom Stores
    * Make sure you're labeling your TikTok Shop Orders with an `order_tag` and this has been communicated with our team.
