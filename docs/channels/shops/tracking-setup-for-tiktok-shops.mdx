Northbeam requires **proper TikTok Shop binding and permissions** in order to attribute GMV and revenue. Ad spend may appear even if binding is incorrect, but revenue will not.

## 1. Bind Your TikTok Shop to Ads Manager

1. Log into **TikTok Ads Manager** (not Business Center).
2. Go to **Assets > TikTok Shop**.
3. Confirm the Shop is **bound directly under the advertiser account**.
   * Shops bound only via Business Center will not sync.

## 2. Connect TikTok Ads Account to Northbeam & Verify Permissions

1. Make sure your TikTok advertiser account is connected to **Northbeam**.
2. Ensure the TikTok user connecting to Northbeam is an **Admin** on both:
   * The TikTok Shop
   * The TikTok advertiser account

## 3. Store-Specific Setup

### ✅ Shopify Stores

* Connect your Shopify store to [TikTok Shops](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/tiktok/setup).
* Ensure your TikTok Shop orders are synced to your Shopify dashboard.
* Ensure your [Northbeam Pixel](/docs/add-pixel) and [Purchase Events](/docs/add-pixel) are properly set up (completed during onboarding).

### ⚙️ Custom Stores

* Use an **Order Tag** to identify TikTok Shop orders.
* Pass the tag in the `order_tags` field via the [Northbeam Server API](/reference/post_orders)
* [Notify our team](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) with the name of the tag.
