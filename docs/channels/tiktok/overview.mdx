**This guide will show you how to set up tracking for the following TikTok metrics in Northbeam:**

* Spend, impressions, visits and all conversion-based metrics attributed to TikTok Ads

<Table align={["left","left","left"]}>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>
        Input
      </th>

      <th>
        Output
      </th>

      <th>
        If Input is missing...
      </th>
    </tr>
  </thead>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>
        TikTok Integration (required)
      </td>

      <td>
        The following data is pulled into your dashboard:

        * Campaign, Adset, Ad Names
        * Ad Spend
        * Impressions
        * TikTok-reported metrics
      </td>

      <td>
        Campaign, Adset, Ad Names will be missing, along with Spend, Impressions, and TikTok-reported metrics.
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        Northbeam UTM Parameters (required)
      </td>

      <td>
        Revenue, Transactions, and Visit attribution to your Campaigns, Adsets, and Ads.
      </td>

      <td>
        Northbeam UTM Parameters create the linkage between your Ads and the conversions they drive. If missing, your ad spend and your revenue will be reported on different line-items, making it very difficult to draw insights.
      </td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</Table>

<Info>
  ### Advertise on TikTok Shop?

  * **Shopify Stores** - Make sure your store is connected to [TikTok Shops](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/tiktok/setup).
  * **Custom Stores** - Make sure to label your TikTok Shop orders with an `order_tag`([Server AP](https://northbeam-orders-api.readme.io/reference/post_orders)I) and [reach out to our team](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) with the name of the tag.

  **For more information,[learn about TikTok Shops (and Meta Shops) here](/docs/tracking-for-shops).**
</Info>
