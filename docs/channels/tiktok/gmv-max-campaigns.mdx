TikTok’s GMV Max is an automated advertising solution designed to maximize a seller’s total sales revenue (GMV) within their TikTok Shop. It uses AI to optimize campaign performance — automating tasks like audience targeting, bidding, and creative selection — while focusing on hitting a target ROI and driving incremental sales through a mix of paid and organic traffic.

In short, GMV Max takes over the heavy lifting of campaign management so you can scale your TikTok Shop sales more efficiently.

## How to track GMV Max in Northbeam:

**Requirements**

* Your TikTok Shop account must be connected to a TikTok Ad Account
* That TikTok Ad Account must also be connected to Northbeam
* The connected user must be an admin on the TikTok Ad Account and TikTok Shops account.

<Tip>
  Once these connections are in place, Northbeam will automatically attribute GMV Max campaign data to the corresponding TikTok campaigns.
</Tip>

## Where to Find GMV Max Performance in Northbeam

Navigate to the Sales page in Northbeam. You'll see a platform labeled **GMV Max**. Within this platform, you’ll find your GMV Max campaigns along with attributed conversion data.

## Troubleshooting GMV Max

**If you don’t see GMV Max spend or attributed conversion data, it’s likely due to one of the following:**

* Your TikTok Shop account isn’t connected to any TikTok Ad Account
* Your TikTok Shop account is connected to a TikTok Ad Account that isn't linked to Northbeam

**To verify this setup in TikTok Business Center:**

1. Select the correct Business Center
2. Go to Assets → Ad Accounts
3. Select the relevant Ad Account
4. Navigate to Assets → TikTok Shop
   1. If your shop isn’t listed, it’s not linked to that Ad Account
