In your Ad Account Settings, add the following two custom parameters. These will apply across all campaigns in the account.

1. Log in to your **Pinterest Business account**
2. Click the hamburger icon (top-left) → **Business Manager**
3. Select **Ad Accounts** → choose your ad account
4. Click the **Settings** tab → **Parameter Settings**
5. Under **Custom Parameters**, add the following:

| Parameter Name | Value                                           |
| :------------- | :---------------------------------------------- |
| `nbt`          | `nb:pinterest::{campaignid}:{adgroupid}:{adid}` |
| `nb_platform`  | `pinterest`                                     |

<Tip>
  **Note:** Pinterest includes standard GA UTMs (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content) by default. These are **not required by Northbeam** and can be removed.
</Tip>

<Note>
  UTM Auto-Appending

  If you would like Northbeam to automatically append UTM parameters to newly created Pinterest ads from ad accounts connected to your dashboard, please [reach out to our Support team](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) to enable this feature.
</Note>
