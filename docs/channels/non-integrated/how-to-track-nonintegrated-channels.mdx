      <td>
        Standard UTMs  

        *(ex. utm\_source, medium, campaign)*
      </td>

      <td>
        Our pixel captures standard UTM parameters (commonly used with Google Analytics) to track visit and conversion data from any platforms that are not directly integrated.  

        The standard UTM parameters include:  

        utm\_source\
        utm\_medium\
        utm\_campaign  

        You have the flexibility to assign any values you prefer.  

        For example, if you wanted to track performance on Criteo, you might use:  

        utm\_source=paid\
        utm\_medium=criteo\
        utm\_campaign=prospecting\_campaign
      </td>
