<Tip>
  What is a Non-Integrated Channel?

  A **Non-Integrated Channel** is any marketing platform not natively connected to Northbeam. Typically, these are platforms not listed under Settings > Connections.

  **Common examples:**

  * Email/SMS Platforms (e.g., Yotpo, Omnisend, GetResponse, Sendlane, Mailchimp)
  * Note: Klaviyo and Attentive have their own setup guides, but spend still needs to be uploaded manually.
  * Influencer Marketing
  * Podcasts
  * Direct Mail
  * Reddit
  * ShareASale
  * Criteo
  * StackAdapt
  * PepperJam
  * Parternize
  * Taboola
  * Commission Junction
  * DV360
</Tip>
