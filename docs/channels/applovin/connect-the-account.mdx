## Step 1. Report API Key


1. Log in to **AppLovin** using your **master user account** (sub-user accounts won’t work).
2. Open the top-right menu → **Keys**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/readme/0f037fe4-image_133.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

3. Copy the **Report Key** (this is your API Reporting Key).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/readme/cf8c95fa-image_130.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

***

## Step 2: Find Your Account ID


1. Log in to **AppLovin** using your **master user account** (sub-user accounts won’t work).
2. Open the top-right menu → **Other Settings**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/readme/816af729-image_131.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

3. Copy your **Account ID**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/readme/e3122de7-image_132.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

***

## Step 3: Add Your Credentials in Northbeam


1. Go to **Settings > Connections** in your Northbeam dashboard.
2. Find the **AppLovin** section.
3. Enter your **Account ID** and **Report API Key**.

![](/images/readme/6228c83e-image.png)
