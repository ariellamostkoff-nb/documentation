## Step 1: Connect Your Universal Ads Account


Northbeam connects to Universal Ads using a standard OAuth connection flow.

* **Onboarding:** Go to **Integrations > Universal Ads**
* **Dashboard:** Go to **Settings > Connections > Universal Ads**

Click **Connect**, then follow the Universal Ads authentication flow to authorize your account.

Once connected successfully, your Universal Ads account will appear in the Connections page.
