If you decide to upload costs via Custom Spendsheet, the Spend data will be categorized under the platform called "Other". We can use our [Breakdowns Manager](/docs/tracking-for-other-platforms#part-3-re-label-custom-spend-via-labels) to move it into "Klaviyo".
