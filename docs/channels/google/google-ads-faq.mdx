Reference our step-by-step guide below:

### Adding an Extra Google Ads Account

Google Ads lets you manage multiple ad accounts under one **Manager Account (MCC)**. Before adding an extra account in Northbeam, you'll need your Manager Account ID handy.

<Warning>
  **Before you start:**

  * All Google Ads accounts you want to connect to a single Northbeam dashboard must be under the same Manager Account.
  * The user shown next to **Connected Account** in your [Connections Page](https://dashboard.northbeam.io/settings/connections) is the one who authenticated the integration. This person must have **Admin access** to both the Manager Account and each individual account being added. Without this, the connection will fail.

### Step 1: Find your Manager Account ID

Log into Google Ads and go to **Accounts**. Your Manager Account ID is the number in the top header (e.g. `651-XXX-XXXX`).

<Frame caption="Log into Manager Account > Accounts">
  <img src="https://files.readme.io/b0be611a36d3c127b116e753abedc5452fb1801e5986a8e39a6c91a73eaaf1e7-CleanShot_2026-04-20_at_15.51.17.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

***

### Step 2: Update the MCC ID in Northbeam

1. Go to **Settings > Connections > Google Ads**
2. In the field labeled **"Update your login customer id (parent MCC account)"**, replace the existing value with your MCC Account ID.
3. Click **Save**
4. Once saved, click **Add another account** to expand the search panel
5. Search for and select the account you want to add
6. Confirm the connection

<Frame>
  <img src="https://files.readme.io/30a23fbb03a1f5dd9e7733e236705e50b226dbd4c3ace9fc5fc5e66c2d4571fa-CleanShot_2026-04-20_at_15.59.15.png" alt="" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  **Seeing a warning?**

  It usually means your Manager Account ID doesn't match the account you're trying to add. Double-check the ID and try again.
</Note>

***

<br />

### Removing an Account

To remove a connected account, click the **Delete** button next to the account name in the Connections panel.

## 2. Why do I see a data discrepancy?

**Discrepancies in Revenue, Orders, and Visits are expected.** The reason for this lies in the differences between Northbeam's attribution model and the corresponding platform's attribution model. Each attribution model has a unique method of allocating credit.

For example, Northbeam's Clicks-Only model divides credit across all touchpoints and tries to push credit to your upper funnel channels. Let's say your in-platform model uses Last Click, which gives credit to the last touchpoint in the journey. This is going to cause a discrepancy.

<DiscrepancyExplanation />

### Attribution Models and Windows

**Google:**

* Attribution Model: Last Click (default)
* Attribution Window: 30-Day Click (default)
  * [Source: Google Conversion Windows](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3123169?hl=en)

**Northbeam:**

* Attribution Model: Varies (default is Clicks-Only)
* Attribution Window: 1-Day (default, can extend to an infinite lifetime value)
