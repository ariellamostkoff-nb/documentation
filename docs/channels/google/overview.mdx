**This guide will show you how to set up tracking for the following Google and YouTube metrics in Northbeam:**

* Spend, impressions, visits and all conversion-based metrics attributed to Google Ads

<Table align={["left","left","left"]}>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>
        Input
      </th>

      <th>
        Output
      </th>

      <th>
        If Input is missing...
      </th>
    </tr>
  </thead>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>
        Northbeam UTM Parameters
      </td>

      <td>
        Revenue, Transactions, and Visit attribution to your Campaigns, Adsets, and Ads.
      </td>

      <td>
        Northbeam UTM Parameters create the linkage between your Ads and the conversions they drive. If missing, your ad spend and your revenue will be reported on different line-items, making it very difficult to draw insights.
      </td>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>
        Google Integration
      </td>

      <td>
        The following data is pulled into your dashboard:

        * Campaign, Adset, Ad Names
        * Ad Spend
        * Impressions
        * Google-reported metrics
      </td>

      <td>
        Campaign, Adset, Ad Names will be missing, along with Spend, Impressions, and Google-reported metrics.
      </td>
    </tr>

</Table>
