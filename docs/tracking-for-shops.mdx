# What are Web Shops, Meta Shops, and TikTok Shops?

We report conversions from three different types of **Shops**.  Each Shop corresponds to a specific checkout experience, or **where** your customer places their order.

| Shop Type    | Description                               |
| :----------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| Web Shops    | Orders placed through your Web Storefront |
| Meta Shops   | Orders placed through Meta Shops          |
| TikTok Shops | Orders placed through TikTok Shops        |

## Web Shops

This is your store's normal checkout experience, where our [Purchase Event](/docs/add-pixel) fires.

## Meta Shops

This is Facebook's (and Instagram's) native shopping experience -- where customers purchase directly from the platform, as opposed to your online store. [Learn about Meta Shops here](https://www.facebook.com/business/shops).

## TikTok Shops

This is TikTok's native shopping experience -- where customers purchase directly from TikTok's platform, as opposed to your online store. [Learn more about TikTok Shops here](https://shop.tiktok.com/business/us).

<Warning>
  Meta Shops and TikTok Shops attribution are only supported using the Clicks Only or the Clicks + Modeled Views attribution models.

  If using First Touch, Last Touch, Last Non-Direct Touch, or Linear -- Meta and TikTok Shops will report as its own line item. You'll simply see total orders and revenue from these channels.
</Warning>

# Tracking Setup for Meta Shops

<br />

## 1. Connect Your Meta Shop to Ads Manager

1. Log into **Meta Ads Manager** with your advertiser account.
2. Go to **Commerce Manager > Shops**.
3. Confirm your Shop is connected directly to the advertiser account.

## 2. Connect Meta Ads Account to Northbeam & Verify Permissions

1. Make sure your Meta advertiser account is connected to **Northbeam**.
2. Ensure the Meta Business user connecting to Northbeam is an **Admin** on both:
   * The Meta Shop (via Commerce Manager)
   * The Meta advertiser account (via Business Manager / Ads Manager)

## 3. Store-Specific Setup

### ✅ Shopify Stores

* Connect your Shopify store to [Facebook and Instagram by Meta](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/facebook-instagram-by-meta/setup).
* Ensure your Meta Shop orders are synced to your Shopify dashboard.
* Ensure your [Northbeam Pixel](/docs/add-pixel) and [Purchase Events](/docs/add-pixel) are properly set up (completed during onboarding).

### ⚙️ Custom Stores

* Use an **Order Tag** to identify Meta Shop orders.
* Pass the tag in the `order_tags` field via the [Northbeam Server API](/reference/post_orders).
* [Reach out to our team](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) with the name of the tag so we can finalize setup.

<br />

# Meta Shops Troubleshooting

* **Ad spend appears but no revenue:**
  * Confirm the Shop is connected directly in **Commerce Manager** and linked to your advertiser account.
  * Confirm the Meta user is an **Admin** on both the Shop and advertiser account.
  * Confirm the Meta advertiser account is properly connected to Northbeam.
  * For Shopify Stores
    * Make sure your store is connected to your shop via [Facebook and Instagram by Meta](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/facebook-instagram-by-meta/setup) app
  * For Custom Stores
    * Make sure you're labeling your Meta Shop Orders with an `order_tag` and this has been communicated with our team.

# Tracking Setup for TikTok Shops

Northbeam requires **proper TikTok Shop binding and permissions** in order to attribute GMV and revenue. Ad spend may appear even if binding is incorrect, but revenue will not.

## 1. Bind Your TikTok Shop to Ads Manager

1. Log into **TikTok Ads Manager** (not Business Center).
2. Go to **Assets > TikTok Shop**.
3. Confirm the Shop is **bound directly under the advertiser account**.
   * Shops bound only via Business Center will not sync.

## 2. Connect TikTok Ads Account to Northbeam & Verify Permissions

1. Make sure your TikTok advertiser account is connected to **Northbeam**.
2. Ensure the TikTok user connecting to Northbeam is an **Admin** on both:
   * The TikTok Shop
   * The TikTok advertiser account

## 3. Store-Specific Setup

### ✅ Shopify Stores

* Connect your Shopify store to [TikTok Shops](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/tiktok/setup).
* Ensure your TikTok Shop orders are synced to your Shopify dashboard.
* Ensure your [Northbeam Pixel](/docs/add-pixel) and [Purchase Events](/docs/add-pixel) are properly set up (completed during onboarding).

### ⚙️ Custom Stores

* Use an **Order Tag** to identify TikTok Shop orders.
* Pass the tag in the `order_tags` field via the [Northbeam Server API](/reference/post_orders)
* [Notify our team](https://www.northbeam.io/submit-a-support-ticket) with the name of the tag.

<br />

# TikTok Shops Troubleshooting

* **Ad spend appears but no GMV/revenue:**
  * Confirm the Shop is **bound in Ads Manager** (not Business Center).
  * Confirm the TikTok user is an **Admin** on both the Shop and advertiser account.
  * Confirm the TikTok advertiser account is properly connected to Northbeam.
  * For Shopify Stores
    * Make sure your store is connected to your shop via [TikTok Shops](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/tiktok/setup) app
  * For Custom Stores
    * Make sure you're labeling your TikTok Shop Orders with an `order_tag` and this has been communicated with our team.

# Meta Shops FAQ

## 1. How do you track and attribute Meta Shops conversions?

Northbeam identifies individual Meta Shops purchases and fractionalizes purchase credit to all touchpoints in the customer journey that led to that purchase.

The major advantage of using Northbeam for Meta Shops attribution is that, unlike Meta, we can link Meta Shops purchases to even non-Meta campaign touchpoints; this way, customers get a true sense of how all paid touchpoints (including non-Meta campaigns) are driving Meta Shops purchase activity.

<Note>
  Note

  **The Northbeam Pixel doesn't fire on Meta Shops storefronts, so we don't track Visits.** Consequently, the Visits count won't be recorded, and CPCs (Cost Per Clicks) may appear inflated for any Campaigns, Ad Sets, or Ads leading to Meta Shops.
</Note>

## 2. How can I view Meta Shops performance?

Meta Shops purchase credit is seamlessly factored into your Attributed Revenue and Transactions metric columns.

To see the revenue or transaction breakout across Web and Shops orders:

1. Hover over any cell in the **Attributed Rev** or **Transactions** columns:

2. Click **Add as columns** to automatically insert new, sortable metric columns into the Sales Table that display the breakout between Web and Shops orders.

  Pro Tip:

  You could also break out your Web Shops, Meta Shops, and TikTok Shops revenue as custom columns by clicking **Customize Table**.
</Tip>

## 3. How can I view Meta Shops organic performance?

Sometimes customers visit your Shops platform to make direct purchases without having clicked on an ad - we refer to these as Organic purchases. Northbeam uses a combination of statistics, platform data, and your ad’s performance to produce an estimate of Organic purchases made on your Shops storefront.

You can see Organic Shops data using the **Meta Shops (Organic)** Platform label.

<Frame caption="Meta Shops (Organic) in the Platform breakdown">
  <img src="https://files.readme.io/1a62d12-Screen_Shot_2024-05-01_at_10.45.50_AM.png" alt="Meta Shops (Organic) in the Platform breakdown" />
</Frame>

## 4. Can I see platform-reported Meta Shops metrics?

Yes, you can!  In the **Sales Page**, under **Customize Table**, find the following metrics under the **Facebook Reported**:

* FB Meta Shop Purchases (1D Click)
* FB Meta Shop Purchases (1D View)
* FB Meta Shop Purchases (7D Click)
* FB Meta Shop Purchases (7D Click, 1D View)
* FB Meta Shop Purchase Conversion Value (1D Click)
* FB Meta Shop Purchase Conversion Value (1D View)
* FB Meta Shop Purchase Conversion Value (7D Click)
* FB Meta Shop Purchase Conversion Value (7D Click, 1D View)
