Northbeam is unique from many internal systems and solutions for several reasons:

* We help you interpret your **first-party data** clearly and confidently so you know exactly what's driving your results.
* Our **proprietary machine learning models** can automatically connect the dots between your campaigns and outcomes to uncover any insights that you may have missed from traditional reporting.
* We offer **built-in tools and features** designed specifically to help you find the most **profitable and scalable strategies** for better decision-making.

The **most powerful feature** in our toolkit is our [Clicks-Only](/docs/attribution-models#clicks-only) and [Clicks + Modeled Views](/docs/attribution-models#clicks--views) attribution models.

* These models **intentionally pull attribution credit away** from bottom-of-funnel channels like *branded search*, *organic*, and *Email/SMS*, and instead **attribute credit to only factual clicks**.
* This method helps you make more directional and unbiased decisions, especially when managing top-of-funnel strategies.

See below for an **example** of a customer's journey with a $100 purchase:

* This is viewed through **Clicks-Only** attribution in **Accrual Performance** mode.
* Instead of giving 100% credit to the last touchpoint (Email), Northbeam attributes the revenue treated in a Linear, Click-Based Fashion.
* We still attribute spend to Email, but it is treated as neutral, lower-funnel assist and not minimally credited.

This ensures that the credit goes to the **touchpoint that initiated and influenced the journey**, rather than the only one that closed the loop.
